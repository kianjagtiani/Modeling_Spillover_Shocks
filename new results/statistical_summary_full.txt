SECTION 1 — RESEARCH QUESTION & HYPOTHESES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Primary research question:
  Do signals from other asset classes — traditional markets (equities, commodities,
  macro) and other crypto assets — contain predictive information about Bitcoin's
  realized volatility, beyond what BTC's own historical volatility already tells us?

Null hypothesis (H₀):
  No asset outside Bitcoin's own price history improves the forecast of BTC
  realized volatility in an out-of-sample, walk-forward setting.

Alternative hypothesis (H₁):
  At least one external asset or seasonal factor provides statistically significant
  incremental predictive power over BTC's own autoregressive history.

Statistical test used:
  Granger causality (AIC-optimal lag selection, F-test, 1921 obs)
  Rejection threshold: p < 0.05 (two-tailed)

KEY FINDING ON DIRECTION:
  For almost every traditional market asset, BTC volatility moves FIRST and the
  traditional asset reacts AFTERWARD — not the other way around. This directly
  contradicts the common assumption that equity market fear (VIX) drives Bitcoin.

  The one asset that shows genuine bi-directional influence: ETH. ETH volatility
  Granger-causes BTC volatility (5-day lag, p=0.005), while BTC and ETH are also
  highly correlated simultaneously (r=0.563). Both assets share the same market
  microstructure, traders, and news cycle.

  S&P 500 RETURNS (not vol) show a 5-day lead on BTC vol (r=−0.08, p<0.001),
  meaning a S&P sell-off weakly predicts higher BTC vol 5 days later. This is
  the only traditional-asset directional signal that holds up.

WHY THE EXPANDED FEATURES HURT:
  The Granger causality results explain this directly. Of the 105 new features added,
  none reached statistical significance for Granger causality. The LightGBM residual
  stage in the hybrid is effectively learning noise from these features, causing
  overfitting that reduces OOS accuracy. This is the curse of dimensionality in action:
  adding uninformative features to an ML model dilutes the signal.

  The marginal exception: within the 55-feature set that DID work, the 5 traditional
  assets (VIX, S&P 500, Gold, DXY, 10Y yield) contributed a small improvement over
  own-history alone (MSE 0.2862 → 0.2858 in earlier ablation). This effect is
  real but economically small, concentrated in the high-vol regime.

SECTION 11 — KEY CONCLUSIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. THE FORECASTING MODEL WORKS WELL
   The HAR-LGBM Hybrid explains 59% of day-to-day BTC vol variation (R²=0.594),
   a 22.8% QLIKE improvement over the academic HAR baseline, statistically
   significant by Diebold-Mariano test (p<0.001). Forecasts are unbiased
   (Mincer-Zarnowitz α≈0, p=0.956).

2. THE MAIN RESEARCH QUESTION: OTHER CRYPTO YES, TRADITIONAL MARKETS NO
   H₀ is rejected for crypto cross-assets (ETH, SOL, BNB vol — all p<0.05).
   H₀ cannot be rejected for any traditional market (VIX p=0.21, S&P p=0.22,
   Gold p=0.18, DXY p=0.58, bonds p=0.45). Traditional markets do not
   Granger-cause BTC volatility in this dataset.

3. DIRECTION SURPRISE: BTC LEADS TRADITIONAL MARKETS
   In the CCF analysis, BTC volatility leads VIX by 1 day, S&P vol by 4 days,
   the dollar by 13–15 days. The conventional narrative (TradFi fear → BTC fear)
   is backward in this data. Bitcoin increasingly moves first.

4. VIX IS REGIME-CONDITIONAL, NOT STRUCTURAL
   VIX only predicts BTC vol in high/normal vol regimes (p<0.001 when stressed,
   p=0.786 when calm). This is a stress-transmission channel, not a persistent
   structural link. In calm markets, the VIX-BTC relationship disappears.

5. ETH IS THE STRONGEST CROSS-ASSET PREDICTOR
   ETH volatility Granger-causes BTC volatility across all three regimes
   (High p<0.001, Normal p<0.001, Low p=0.005). The lead time is 2 days.
   ETH is a better BTC vol predictor than any traditional market asset.

6. MORE FEATURES HURT THE MODEL
   Expanding from 55 to 160 features (adding NDX/DJI/sectors/commodities/
   DeFi/seasonality) degraded QLIKE by 23% (0.0444 → 0.0546). The Granger
   causality results explain why: the added features are statistically
   uninformative, and the ML model overfits to them in-sample.

7. THE STRATEGY IS A RISK MANAGEMENT TOOL, NOT ALPHA
   Vol-timing is statistically non-random (permutation p=0.002). But the
   strategy does not generate significantly higher returns than buy-and-hold
   (NW p=0.996). Its consistent edge is drawdown reduction: −41.7% vs −71.5%
   in backtests, and 100% of simulated paths show smaller worst-case losses.
   Set expectations accordingly: this is portfolio protection, not return generation.

8. BLACK SWANS REMAIN UNPREDICTABLE
   The 5 worst prediction errors (FTX, yen carry unwind, Iran-Israel, ATH surge,
   post-FTX recovery) all involve information that is not in any of the 160
   features. No feature set catches these. The forecast degrades exactly where
   risk management matters most.
