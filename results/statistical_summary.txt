OVERVIEW
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Evaluation period : 2022-01-28 → 2026-04-06
  Trading days      : 1,530
  Forecast horizon  : 1-day ahead (h=1)
  Validation method : Sliding walk-forward (365-day window, 5-day embargo)
  Target variable   : Log realized volatility from 5-min intraday returns
  Annualization     : sqrt(365) for 24/7 crypto markets
  Data sources      : Binance.US (crypto), yfinance (traditional assets),
                      alternative.me (Fear & Greed Index)

FEATURE GROUPS  (54 total features)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Autoregressive  : log(RV) lags 1d/5d/22d, signed jumps, realized
                    semi-variances (upside/downside), vol-of-vol, RV percentile
  Crypto cross-asset: ETH, SOL, BNB vol & returns; BTC-ETH correlation;
                    BTC relative volume
  Traditional assets: VIX level/avg/change/spike, log(VIX)
                    S&P 500 returns, 5d/22d vol, Parkinson range vol
                    Gold returns & 5d vol
                    DXY returns & 5d vol (negative predictor)
                    10Y Treasury yield level & 5d change
  Seasonality     : Day-of-week, month, day-of-year (sin/cos encoded);
                    halving cycle phase; weekend & US holiday dummies;
                    quarter-end dummy
  Sentiment       : Fear & Greed index, extreme dummies, 7d change

MODELS EVALUATED
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  1. HAR-RV-SJ          — OLS baseline (daily/weekly/monthly lags + jumps)
  2. HAR-Lasso          — Regularized HAR with all 54 features
  3. XGBoost            — Gradient boosted trees, full feature set
  4. LightGBM           — Fast gradient boosting, full feature set
  5. HAR-LGBM Hybrid    — HAR (Stage 1) + LightGBM on residuals (Stage 2)
  6. HAR-LGBM Tuned     — HAR-LGBM Hybrid with Optuna hyperparameter tuning
  7. HAR-LSTM Hybrid    — HAR (Stage 1) + 2-layer LSTM on residuals (Stage 2)
  8. Stacking Ensemble  — Ridge meta-learner over all base model predictions

KEY FINDINGS
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  1. HAR-LGBM Hybrid remains the best model across all metrics.
  2. Adding VIX, S&P 500, Gold, DXY, and 10Y yields provided marginal
     improvement overall but is most impactful during high-vol regimes.
  3. VIX is the strongest traditional predictor — leads BTC vol by 1-3 days.
  4. DXY is a negative predictor: dollar strength suppresses BTC volatility.
  5. Pure ML models (XGBoost, LSTM standalone) still underperform HAR hybrids.
  6. Backtest: Combined vol-sizing + regime-filter strategy achieves Sharpe 0.56
     vs 0.02 for buy-and-hold, with max drawdown reduced from -71% to -42%.
  7. Monte Carlo: Strategy's primary durable advantage is risk reduction —
     drawdown cut holds in 100% of simulated paths. Return alpha is inconsistent.
  8. Overfitting risk: IS→OOS Sharpe gap of 0.71 shows parameter optimization
     memorizes historical regimes. Keep strategy simple (inverse-vol, low target).
  9. Model cannot predict black swan events regardless of feature set —
     FTX, geopolitical shocks, and exchange-specific events remain unpredictable.
